Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

This application ("the App") is a personal tool operated by Omega Industries that publishes the owner's own videos to the owner's TikTok and YouTube accounts.

Data we access

With the account holder's permission (via TikTok and Google sign-in), the App accesses basic profile information and the ability to upload videos to the connected account.

How data is used

This data is used only to publish the owner's videos and check their publishing status. It is not sold, shared with third parties for marketing, or used for advertising.

Storage

Access tokens are stored securely by our integration provider and can be revoked at any time from the TikTok or Google account settings.

Deletion
